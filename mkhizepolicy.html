Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 8 November 2026

Welcome to Mkhize Inventory. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Mkhize Inventory collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our inventory management application.

By accessing or using Mkhize Inventory, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, Mkhize Inventory may collect or process the following categories of information:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, username, and authentication details used to manage your account.
  • Inventory Information: Product names, descriptions, stock quantities, prices, categories, product images, and SKU numbers.
  • Transaction Information: Purchases, sales, stock movements, inventory adjustments, and related records.
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, application version, and technical diagnostic information where available.
  • Usage Information: Application activity and interactions necessary to maintain and improve the service.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information processed through Mkhize Inventory may be used for the following purposes:

  • To provide inventory management services.
  • To create and manage user accounts.
  • To authenticate authorized users.
  • To monitor product quantities and stock levels.
  • To record purchases, sales, and stock movements.
  • To generate inventory reports and summaries.
  • To synchronize and maintain inventory records.
  • To identify errors and improve application performance.
  • To protect the application against unauthorized access.

3. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss, or destruction.

Depending on the application's configuration, information may be stored locally on your device or on secure remote servers.

Access to information should be limited to authorized users and personnel who require it to provide or maintain the application services.

However, no electronic storage system or internet transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent users' personal information to third parties.

Information may be shared or processed by third parties only when reasonably necessary for purposes such as:

  • Providing application hosting and infrastructure.
  • Authentication and account management.
  • Application maintenance and technical support.
  • Complying with applicable legal requirements.
  • Protecting the security and rights of users.
  • Fulfilling requests authorized by the user.

5. Third-Party Services

Mkhize Inventory may integrate third-party services for hosting, authentication, analytics, backups, or other application functionality.

These third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and security practices.

Users are encouraged to review the privacy policies of relevant third-party services where applicable.

6. Application Permissions

Depending on the functionality enabled in the application, Mkhize Inventory may request access to certain device permissions.

  • Camera: May be used to scan product barcodes or capture product images, if supported.
  • Photos and Storage: May be used to upload product images or access inventory documents, if supported.
  • Internet Access: Used to connect to online services and synchronize information where required.

Permissions are requested when necessary for the corresponding application features. Users can manage available permissions through their device settings.

7. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide the application services, maintain relevant business records, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

When information is no longer required, reasonable steps may be taken to delete or anonymize it, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

8. Children's Privacy

Mkhize Inventory is designed as an inventory management application and is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without appropriate authorization. If such information is identified, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable privacy and data protection laws, users may have the right to:

  • Request access to their personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of personal information.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Ask how their personal information is being processed.
  • Raise concerns about the handling of their information.

To exercise these rights, users can contact us using the email address provided below.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy when necessary to reflect changes in application features, data handling practices, security measures, or applicable legal requirements.

Any updates will be published on this page with a revised Last Updated date.

Users are encouraged to review this page periodically to remain informed about how their information is handled.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, complaints, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the handling of your personal information, please contact us.
